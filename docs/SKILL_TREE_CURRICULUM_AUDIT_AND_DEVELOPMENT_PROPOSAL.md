### Headline Findings
1. **Strong Architectural Bones & Theory Spine:** The 15-skill Theory & Knowledge expansion and the `standardVersion: 2` rewrites (`Walk`, `Trot`, `Canter`, `Transitions`, `Half-Halt`) set an exceptionally clear, humane, cross-discipline benchmark.
2. **Uneven Educational Depth Between New and Earlier Files:** While `Theory & Knowledge` and the upgraded mounted gait skills articulate *"what it looks and feels like,"* *"common mistakes,"* and *"welfare notes,"* most earlier practical skills (especially `Horse → In-Hand`, non-gait `Horse → Mounted`, and `Rider → Mounted` pivots/lateral work) average only **1.09–1.42 components per level** and **1.00–1.38 safety notes per skill**.
3. **Critical Relationship Graph Gaps Before Recommendations:** Only **5 of 57** practical skills carry explicit `"relation": "theory"` links, and **19 of 22** Horse skills contain only a single `counterpart` link with zero internal `foundation` or `next` progression links. Populating this knowledge graph is essential before personalized recommendations can add genuine value.
4. **Targeted Foundational Skill & Level Gaps:** A handful of glaring general-horsemanship capabilities are missing—most notably **Rider Seat, Position & Independent Balance**, **Emergency Control & Balance Recovery**, and several missing **Horse → In-Hand** counterparts (**Longeing**, **In-Hand Obstacles & Outings**, **In-Hand Yielding & Body Control**)—alongside steep single-level jumps in **Leading** (trot in `Intro`), **Longeing** (canter in first level), **First Aid**, **Rein Back**, and **Leg Yield**.
5. **Minor Existing Documentation Drift Observed:**
   - [`AGENTS.md`](file:///home/matteo/Pictures/Projects/Horse-and-Rider-s-Companion/AGENTS.md) lists `149` difficulty-level standards from the initial Theory launch; actual `curriculum/v2/` now holds **`154`** levels after commit `b474b2b` added 5 mounted gait levels (`rider.mounted.transitions.introductory` plus `advanced` on Rider and Horse `Trot` and `Canter`).
   - [`docs/SKILL_TREE_CURRICULUM_GUIDE.md`](file:///home/matteo/Pictures/Projects/Horse-and-Rider-s-Companion/docs/SKILL_TREE_CURRICULUM_GUIDE.md) §2.3 describes `Sidepass` inside `Pivots & Body Control`, whereas both `skills_rider_mounted.json` and `skills_horse_mounted.json` actually place `*.mounted.sidepass` inside `*.mounted.lateral_movements` at `sortOrder: 40`. Keeping `*.mounted.sidepass` in `Lateral Movements` and updating the guide text avoids unnecessary re-indexing.

### Preserved Principles & What They Require in This Phase

| Preserved Principle | Source Citation | Concrete Implication for Curriculum Expansion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lifelong, nonlinear learning** | Guide §1, §4.4; Architecture §A.3 | Horses and riders develop along different paths and timelines. Adding deeper knowledge or optional specialties must expand discovery without turning horsemanship into a linear school syllabus. |
| **Capabilities over checklists** | Guide §1, §3; `AGENTS.md` | Every new candidate must pass the **Six-Way Classification Decision Tree** (`Alias` → `Component` → `Variation/Application` → `Difficulty Level` → `New Skill` → `Family`) so tools, arena patterns, and chores stay inside `components[]` and `aliases[]` instead of creating micro-skills. |
| **No percentages or completion scores** | Guide §1, §2.1; `AGENTS.md` | Never add completion percentages, mastery meters, or mandatory quotas per family/category. Adding advanced or specialized content must never make a trail or recreational rider look "less complete." |
| **Suggestions, never locks** | Guide §1, §4.4, §6.1; Architecture §A.4 | Use `foundation`, `next`, `counterpart`, and `theory` links purely for guidance and future recommendations—never to lock skills or block recording progress at any difficulty level. |
| **Two orthogonal dimensions & existing states** | Guide §4.1–4.4; `AGENTS.md` | Keep Difficulty (`Introductory`, `Intermediate`, `Advanced`—only levels with meaningfully different standards, 1–3 per skill) completely separate from Proficiency (`Not Started`, `Learning`, `Proficient`, and independent `Instructor Verified`). |
| **Knowledge, observation, feel & execution all matter** | `AGENTS.md`; Guide §2.2, §6.1 | Practical skills must evaluate feel, timing, preparation, and adjustment—not mechanical posture alone. Theory proficiency measures practical recognition and real-world application, not rote vocabulary or ideology. |
| **Equine welfare, humane training & safety first** | Guide §1, §6.1; `AGENTS.md` | Safety always supersedes speed or gait. Explanations must distinguish pain, fear, or confusion from "disobedience" and treat horses as sentient learners rather than passive objects. |
| **All-equestrian inclusivity & technique neutrality** | `AGENTS.md`; Guide §6.1(12) | Standards apply equally across Western, English, ranch, trail, driving, gaited, therapeutic, and general horsemanship. Specific methods/tack systems belong in linked **Resources**, while **The Training Pyramid** remains an adaptable lens—not the sole doctrine. |
| **Immutable IDs & progress preservation** | Guide §8.1–8.5; `AGENTS.md` | Never rename or delete published `id`s or `familyId`s; preserve all 84 `legacyIds[]`; increment `standardVersion` whenever a `proficient` standard changes materially so prior instructor verifications remain preserved cleanly. |

### 3.7 Single-Skill Families & Content Overlap Audit

#### Single-Skill Families (12 of 32 Families Currently Hold 1 Skill)
Per [`SKILL_TREE_CURRICULUM_GUIDE.md`](file:///home/matteo/Pictures/Projects/Horse-and-Rider-s-Companion/docs/SKILL_TREE_CURRICULUM_GUIDE.md) §2.3 & §9.1, domain-clear 1-skill families are structurally legal—we never merge unrelated domains or split unified skills just to pad counts. However, **three single-skill families fail their own stated scope**:
1. **`rider.mounted.position_balance_safety` (*Position, Balance & Safety*):** Description promises *"maintaining a secure, balanced rider position,"* yet contains only `Mounting & Dismounting` (`Intro`).
2. **`horse.in_hand.travel_environments` (*Travel & New Environments*):** Description promises *"coping calmly with trailers and unfamiliar surroundings,"* yet contains only `Trailer Loading` (missing `In-Hand Obstacles & Outings`).
3. **`horse.in_hand.work_on_line` (*Work on the Line*):** Contains only `Ground Driving` (missing `Longeing` counterpart).

1. **`rider.husbandry.tack` (`Int`) vs. `rider.theory.tack_fit_function` (`Intro`/`Int`):** Both address static/dynamic saddle and bridle fit. **Resolution:** Keep `rider.husbandry.tack` centered on *hands-on daily putting on, pre-ride inspection, adjustments, and maintenance*, while `rider.theory.tack_fit_function` handles *biomechanical/anatomical mechanics (bits, hackamores, tree balance, dynamic sweat cues)*—and cross-link them cleanly.

## 5. Educational Quality Review & Weaknesses

Evaluated from a horse trainer and educator standpoint against all 9 curriculum quality criteria:

### 5.1 Educational Depth & Kinesthetic "Feel" Gap ("Two-Tier Curriculum")
The 15 Theory skills and 5 updated Mounted gait skills average **2.1–2.3 rich components per level** and consistently explain *why the skill matters*, *what mistakes feel like*, and *welfare warning signs*. By contrast, **67 of 154 levels have only 1 component** and **31 of 72 skills have only 1 safety bullet**—concentrated in `Horse → In-Hand` (0/7 mention welfare or common mistakes inside components), `Horse → Mounted` non-gait skills (0/11 beyond gaits/transitions), and `Rider → In-Hand` / `Rider → Mounted` pivots and lateral movements.
- *Missing "Feel & Look" Precision:* Older entries tell the rider *what geometry to ride* (e.g., `"maintaining steady rhythm"` or `"keeping the body parallel"`), without helping a learner recognize **what it actually feels like in the saddle or on the lead** (e.g., feeling the outside shoulder bulge before turning, distinguishing genuine forward leg-crossing from stiff neck-pulling in `Sidepass` or `Travers`, or recognizing tension masked as stillness in `Standing Quietly`).
- *Missing "Common Mistakes":* Pivots (`Turn on the Forehand`, `Turn on the Haunches`), `Rein Back`, `Leg Yield`, `Shoulder-In`, `Travers`, `Longeing`, and Horse In-Hand skills rarely warn learners about predictable mistakes (e.g., pulling backward inside a `Turn on the Forehand`, letting the horse step backward out of `Yield the Forequarters`, twisting on a stuck pivot foot, or habituating a horse by accidentally trapping/flooding it).

### 5.2 Severe Relationship Graph Sparsity
Future personalized discovery and recommendations depend on meaningful `relatedSkills` links. Currently:
- Only **5 of 57** practical skills link to any `rider.theory.*` skill (`relation: "theory"`). None of Husbandry (0/10), Rider In-Hand (0/9), or Horse skills (0/22) carry `theory` links—even though Husbandry maps directly to `pain_discomfort_fatigue`, `tack_fit_function`, and `ethical_training`, and In-Hand maps directly to `how_horses_learn`, `timing_reinforcement`, and `stress_fear_confidence`.
- **19 of 22 Horse skills** have *zero* `foundation` or `next` progression links within Horse (e.g., `horse.in_hand.leading` does not link to `standing_quietly` or `ground_driving`; `horse.mounted.leg_yield` does not link to `shoulder_in`; `horse.mounted.sidepass` does not link to `object_handling`).
- `rider.husbandry.feeding` and `rider.husbandry.stable_care` are completely orphaned (`relatedSkills: []`).
- `rider.theory.session_planning` and `rider.theory.stress_fear_confidence` have zero links connecting them into practical riding or groundwork.

### 5.3 Unrealistic Level Jumps & Bundled Standards
1. **`rider.in_hand.leading` (`Introductory`) vs. `horse.in_hand.leading` (`Intermediate`):** Rider `Intro` bundles trotting beside the horse; a beginner safely leading at walk/halt/turns cannot reach `Introductory` proficiency until they also jog a horse in-hand.
2. **`rider.in_hand.longeing` (`Intermediate`):** The entry level jumps straight to walk, trot, **and canter** in both directions while keeping circle geometry. Safe walk/trot longeing is an important earlier milestone (`Introductory` on a prepared horse, or separating walk/trot `Intermediate` from canter/pole-refinement `Advanced`).
3. **`rider.in_hand.yield_forequarters` (*Turn on the Haunches (In-Hand)*):** Starts at `Intermediate` with no `Introductory` level, creating an asymmetry with `turn_on_forehand` (In-Hand `Intro/Int`) and mounted `Turn on the Haunches` (`Intro/Int`).
4. **`rider.husbandry.first_aid`:** Starts at `Intermediate` only; beginner riders/barn helpers need an `Introductory` standard covering spotting urgent bleeding/swelling/choke/colic, keeping themselves safe, knowing what *not* to alter (puncture nails, deep joint wounds, eyes), and fetching help + kit immediately.
5. **`rider.mounted.rein_back` (`Int`, `Adv`) & `horse.mounted.rein_back` (`Int` only):** Both bundle straight flat backing (3–5 steps) *and* steering backward through an L-corridor into their first available level (`Int`), and Horse lacks an `Advanced` standard mirroring Rider.
6. **`rider.mounted.leg_yield` & `horse.mounted.leg_yield`:** Single `Intermediate` standard bundles walk (4 beats—ample time to coordinate inside leg feel) and trot (2-beat suspension—substantially harder rhythm and straightness test).
7. **`rider.mounted.canter` & `horse.mounted.canter` (`Advanced`):** Bundles *both* sustained counter-canter *and* clean simultaneous flying lead changes in both directions into one `Advanced` standard—a huge athletic leap for non-dressage/green horses and recreational riders who develop counter-canter well before flying changes.
8. **`horse.in_hand.vet_handling` (`Int` only) & `horse.in_hand.trailer_loading` (`Int` only):** Both lack `Introductory` stepping stones (owner mouth/eye/ear/thermometer prep; calm walk onto a ramp or into an open trailer without closing doors).

### 5.4 Residual Subject-Boundary Wording (10 Rider Levels + 2 Horse Skills)
Ten Rider `proficient` rubrics still evaluate the **horse's physical/emotional state** rather than purely grading the **rider's preparation, cues, position, timing, and adaptive adjustment on a prepared horse**:
- `rider.in_hand.turn_on_forehand.introductory` (*"and calm relaxation"*)
- `rider.in_hand.sidepass_in_hand.intermediate` (*"keeping the horse's body straight and relaxed"*)
- `rider.in_hand.longeing.advanced` (*"maintaining relaxed responsiveness"*)
- `rider.mounted.trot.advanced` (*"preserving horse relaxation"*)
- `rider.mounted.transitions.advanced` (*"steady relaxation"*)
- `rider.mounted.half_halt.intermediate` (*"improving the horse's posture and responsiveness"*)
- `rider.mounted.turn_on_forehand.intermediate` (*"no backward steps"*)
- `rider.mounted.shoulder_in.intermediate` (*"moving forward along the line without drifting"*) & `.advanced` (*"and relaxation"*)
- `rider.mounted.object_handling.advanced` (*"and calm horse responsiveness"*)

Conversely, two Horse skills drift slightly into human pattern ownership:
- `horse.mounted.steering_navigation` (`Intro`/`Int`): describe following the *rider's directional and bend requests* cleanly through lines/circles/patterns rather than reading as if the horse self-navigates a 20 m circle.
- `horse.mounted.rein_back` (`Int`): frame backing through an L/corridor as responding softly to the rider's aligning shoulder/hip cues without hollowing or bracing.

### 5.5 Safety & Welfare Omissions
Specific safety rules needed inside existing skills:
- `rider.in_hand.longeing`: excessive repetitive tight circles stressing immature/arthritic joints; footing/mud check; never clipping heavy snaps directly to a snaffle ring without bridle safety/cavesson awareness.
- `rider.mounted.mounting`: reading the horse's posture/relaxation *before* mounting; ensuring mounting block stability; re-checking girth after walking a few steps before trotting.
- `rider.husbandry.blanketing`: cooling/drying sweat/rain before putting on non-breathable sheets or heavy turnout rugs.
- `rider.husbandry.feeding`: letting overheated horses cool before heavy concentrates; soaking pelleted/beet-pulp feeds where appropriate to prevent choke; monitoring lush spring pasture/metabolic risks.
- `rider.mounted.turn_on_haunches` & lateral work: avoiding deep, slick, or rocky footing during pivot torque; limiting continuous reps to avoid fatigue/strain.
- `rider.mounted.terrain_obstacles`: explicit up-hill vs. down-hill rider balance safety (staying forward out of the saddle up steep climbs; staying centered and freeing the horse's head/mouth down slopes).
- `horse.in_hand.*`: explicitly noting freeze/shutdown ("learned helplessness") cues so a tense, frozen horse standing still is never mistaken for `Proficient` relaxation.

### 5.6 Cross-Discipline Inclusivity Notes
- **`rider.mounted.trot` (`Introductory`):** Currently requires *"rising (posting) trot"*; Western riders starting in a relaxed slow **jog** sit cleanly without posting—and riders on four-beat **gaited/ambling breeds** do not ride a two-beat trot at all. Broaden `Introductory` wording to welcome balanced rising trot *or* comfortable sitting jog (and recognize gaited/ambling breed applicability notes).
- **`rider.mounted.canter` (`Int`) & `steering_navigation` (`Intro`/`Int`):** Keep `"~20 m"` explicitly illustrative (*"large balanced circles (~20 m)"*) so trail/ranch riders without marked dressage letters aren't confused.
- **Theory `Advanced` Criteria (`mentoring others`):** `training_pyramid`, `session_planning`, `timing_reinforcement`, `ethical_training`, `understanding_rider_aids`, and `training_equipment` reference *"mentoring others"* or teaching in `Advanced`. Independent lifelong learners who do not teach lessons should achieve `Advanced` through deeper diagnostic analysis, cross-context adaptation, and ethical decision-making without mandatory coaching duties.

## 7. Rider vs. Horse Distinctions & Relationship Architecture

### 7.1 Current Balance & Guardrails for Treating Horses as Sentient Partners
Currently, the curriculum tracks **50 Rider skills (109 levels)** vs. **22 Horse skills (45 levels)**. While Husbandry (`10` skills) and Theory (`15` skills) are rightly **Rider-only**, Rider In-Hand (`9`) + Mounted (`16`) = **25 practical skills**, whereas Horse has **22 practical skills** (`7` In-Hand, `15` Mounted).

To ensure **horses are never treated as passive training objects** when upgrading and adding Horse skills:
1. **Grade Mental State Alongside Physical Execution:** Every Horse `Introductory` / `Intermediate` skill must evaluate *emotional composure, softness, curiosity, and willingness* (relaxed breathing, soft eye, willing ear attention)—and explicitly warn against mistaking a shut-down/frozen horse for a calm, `Proficient` horse.
2. **Acknowledge Physical Comfort & Confirmation Limits:** Frame failures or hesitation neutrally (a horse struggling with a canter lead or turn on the haunches may lack strength, asymmetry conditioning, or saddle comfort—not "stubbornness").
3. **Assume Fair, Clear Human Requests:** Horse rubrics evaluate *how the horse responds to light, clear, timely cues*; Rider rubrics evaluate *how the rider prepares, asks, releases, and adjusts on a reasonably prepared horse*.

### 7.2 High-Value Relationship Links to Add (`relatedSkills`)
To power future personalized recommendations without adding rigid locks:
- **Practical → Theory (`"relation": "theory"`):** Wire **50+ links** connecting Husbandry, In-Hand, and Mounted practical skills to relevant `rider.theory.*` concepts:
  - *Safe Handling / Catching / Leading / Standing / Daily Care* ↔ `equine_body_language`, `stress_fear_confidence`, `how_horses_learn`
  - *Yielding (In-Hand) / Trailer Loading / Obstacles / Longeing / Ground Driving* ↔ `timing_reinforcement`, `how_horses_learn`, `training_equipment`
  - *Health Monitoring / Blanketing / Grooming / First Aid / Tack* ↔ `pain_discomfort_fatigue`, `tack_fit_function`, `ethical_training`, `progressive_conditioning`
  - *Walk / Trot / Canter / Transitions / Half-Halt / Steering / Pivots / Lateral / Terrain* ↔ `gaits_footfalls`, `balance_weight`, `bend_flexion_straightness`, `understanding_rider_aids`, `progressive_conditioning`, `session_planning`
- **Internal Horse Progression (`foundation` & `next`):** Connect Horse skills cleanly inside `Horse → In-Hand` and `Horse → Mounted`:
  - `horse.in_hand.leading` → `catching_turnout` / `standing_quietly` / `yielding` / `obstacles_outings` / `trailer_loading` / `longeing` → `ground_driving`
  - `horse.mounted.standing` & `walk` → `transitions` & `steering_navigation` → `turn_on_forehand` → `leg_yield` → `shoulder_in` → `travers`; `turn_on_forehand` + `leg_yield` → `sidepass` → `object_handling`; `steering_navigation` → `terrain_obstacles`

### Tier 2 — Valuable Educational Expansions (General All-Discipline Horsemanship)

1. **`rider.mounted.open_space_group_riding` (*Riding Safely in Open Spaces & Groups*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`) & `horse.mounted.open_space_group_confidence` (*Composure in Open Spaces & Groups*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`):**

2. **`rider.mounted.rein_communication` (*Rein Communication & Connection*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`) & `horse.mounted.softness_connection` (*Softness, Relaxation & Self-Carriage*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`):**
   - *Why distinct:* Bridges a major cross-discipline gap without choosing sides—covering **both** elastic two-handed contact *and* draped/neck-rein communication (`Intro` soft follow without catching mouth/jaw → `Int` shortening/lengthening reins smoothly, gentle flexion/give to pressure, one-handed/neck-rein transitions → `Adv` sustained lightness, self-carriage, and responsive transitions on minimal rein weight).
3. **`rider.theory.rider_biomechanics` (*Rider Biomechanics & Asymmetric Influence*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`)** in `rider.theory.rider_equipment`:
   - *Why distinct:* `balance_weight` examines weight distribution on the *horse*; this covers the *human* body—pelvic tilt, ribcage rotation, breathing, visual focus, natural human asymmetry, stirrup/ankle shock absorption, and how rider tension directly restricts the horse's back and poll.
4. **`rider.theory.safety_risk_assessment` (*Safety, Risk Assessment & Emergency Readiness*, `Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`)** in `rider.theory.training_principles` *(or `behavior`)*:
   - *Why distinct:* Proactive barn/turnout/weather/footing/trailer risk assessment, protective gear (helmets, footwear), recognizing environmental/arousal red flags early, equine fire/evacuation & hauling emergency planning, and deciding responsibly when to end a ride or seek help.
5. **`rider.husbandry.preventive_care` (*Preventive Health, Hoof & Dental Stewardship*, `Intro`, `Int`)** in `rider.husbandry.health_first_aid`:
   - *Why distinct:* Complements daily `health_monitoring` and crisis `first_aid` with routine long-term welfare planning: 4–8 week farrier cycles & healthy hoof alignment literacy, routine dental exams, targeted FEC/parasite stewardship, core/risk vaccination planning, body condition/metabolic prevention, and record keeping.
6. **`rider.mounted.poles_cavaletti` & `horse.mounted.poles_cavaletti` (`Intro`, `Int`, `Adv`):**
   - *Why distinct:* Ground poles and low gymnastic cavaletti grids develop cadence, stride adjustability, back suppleness, core engagement, and rider two-point timing across walk/trot/canter without requiring jumping (`Terrain & Obstacle Negotiation` covers varied outdoor obstacles rather than structured gymnastic striding).
7. **De-Bundling `Counter-Canter & Lead Changes` (`Canter Advanced` Consideration):**
   - Currently `Canter Advanced` demands both counter-canter and flying changes. Splitting **`Counter-Canter & Lead Changes`** into a dedicated Rider + Horse pair (`Int`: simple changes & early counter-canter; `Adv`: sustained counter-canter & straight flying lead changes) gives both intermediate and advanced horses/riders realistic, trackable milestones without blocking `Canter`. *(Presented to Matt as an explicit Tier 2 option.)*
